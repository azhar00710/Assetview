MANIFOLD CONFIGURATION
================================================================================

The manifold connects three well flowlines to the production header and pig launcher:

INLET CONNECTIONS:
  - AF-9 Wellhead: 8"-H-28-12-0122-J26S-P (from Sheet 005)
  - AF-10 Wellhead: 8"-H-28-12-0222-J26S-P (from Sheet 006, also to test manifold)
  - AF-4 Wellhead: 8"-H-28-12-0328-J26S-P (from Sheet 001)

OUTLET:
  - 14" Production Header: 14"-H-28-12-0322-J26S-P → TO NKPP PLATFORM
  - Test Manifold: 8"-H-28-12-0331-J26S-P
  - Pig Launcher GP-2802: For pigging operations

BYPASS:
  - ESD-0 BYPASS with locked valves (V-28121, V-28122)
  - ESD-0 BYPASSED indication

DRIP PAN:
  - Located below pig launcher GP-2802
  - Drains to closed drain header

NOTES
================================================================================
1.  LOW/LOW OR HIGH/HIGH PRESSURE, CLOSE WING AND MASTER VALVES OF EXISTING AND NEW WELLS.
2.  SPOOL PIECE FOR MAINTENANCE PURPOSES.
3.  SIGNAL TO WHPP SWITCHBOARD.
4.  ESV ON RISER TO BE LOCATED IN SAFE AREA AWAY FROM EXTERNAL FIRE OR EXPLOSION SOURCES.
    HOWEVER, ACCESS AND MAINTENANCE FACILITIES TO BE PROVIDED.
5.  METHANOL AND NITROGEN SUPPLY POINTS TO BE CLOSE TO PIG TRAP, 8MM MANIFOLD AFTER
    PIGGING OPERATIONS.
6.  METHANOL INJECTION DEPENDANT ON WELLSTREAM TEMPERATURE AND PRESSURE.
7.  CONNECTION FOR FUTURE WELLHEADS.
8.  BUILT-IN VALVE OF PIG CLOSURE PART OF VENDOR SUPPLY.
9.  TAG NOS. FOR ALL SINDERED HUBS SHALL BE:
    SP-217AIB FOR PIPE CLASS J85S.
    SP-217AXB FOR PIPE CLASS J26S.
10. NOZZLES ON PIG LAUNCHER SHALL BE VERTICAL TO THE TRUE HORIZONTAL PLANE.
11. QUICK OPENING CLOSURE DOOR. PROVIDE SPACE FOR DRIP PAN/NELING DOOR TO BE PROVIDED WITH A
    MECHANICAL DEVICE TO PROTECT AGAINST OPENING UNDER PRESSURE.
12. THE LAUNCHER SHALL BE PROVIDED WITH KEY INTERLOCK SYSTEM TO PREVENT INADVERTENT OPENING
    OF LAUNCHER END CLOSURE UNDER PRESSURE.
13. CLOSURES WELD TO BE 100% RADIOGRAPHED.
14. HUBS 99/893 SCRUB VIA SUBMARINE CABLE.
15. SPOOL PIECE FOR THREE PHASE FUTURE TEST METER ASSEMBLY TO BE PROVIDED ON VERTICAL LEG.
16. LOW AND HIGH RANGE PRESSURE GAUGE.
17. FOR EACH BLIND SPACE CORRESPONDING SPACER SHOULD ALSO BE PROVIDED.
18. TELL TALE CONNECTION TO BE USED FOR CHECKING HCS LEVEL INSIDE PIG LAUNCHER AFTER N2 PURGING.
19. MINIMUM DISTANCE LARGER THAN THE LENGTH OF INTELLIGENT PIG IS REQUIRED BETWEEN BENDS/CSY
    AND FLUSHING TEE.
20. ESV-289950 SHALL BE PROVIDED WITH PARTIAL STROKING FACILITY.
21. LOCATE AS CLOSE AS POSSIBLE TO THE ENDS OF PIG LAUNCHER.
